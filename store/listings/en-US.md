Title (26/30): Rotate 180°: Flip & Charge

Short (63/80): Rotate your phone upside down. Auto 180° and charging controls.

Full description (920/4000):

Turn your phone upside down in portrait mode when the charging cable gets in the way.

Force 180°: manually activate upside-down portrait orientation.

Auto 180°: switch between portrait upright (0°) and upside down (180°). The app does not force landscape at 90° or 270°.

While charging: automatically apply upside-down portrait when you connect a power cable, then return to your previously configured mode when disconnected.

Also includes: Quick Settings tile, optional per-app exclusions and a persistent service notification with a STOP control.

Permissions: Display over other apps is required to apply the orientation. Usage Access is optional and is used only for app exclusions. Notifications and a foreground service support continuous orientation control.

Ad-free, no app account. Functionality depends on Android version and device manufacturer; please check your device permissions and battery settings.

Note: do not claim 90/270 landscape, universal device support or guaranteed background reliability.
